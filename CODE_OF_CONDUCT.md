# Code of Conduct

Linnya contributors and maintainers are expected to make technical collaboration respectful, safe, and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not a person's identity or motives.
- Give concrete evidence and make tradeoffs understandable.
- Welcome good-faith questions and different levels of experience.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and confidential reports.
- Accept maintainer decisions without harassment; disagreement may continue through a scoped issue or proposal.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, impersonation, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions consider severity, repetition, safety, and the project's ability to collaborate effectively.

For public incidents, use the platform's report or block controls and link the relevant public content in a maintainer-facing issue when safe. Do not publish private personal information in an issue. Security vulnerabilities must use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
